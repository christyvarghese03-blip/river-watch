04Sources

Where Every Number Comes From

  • Team Sheet (town, resources, overrides, notes): Google Sheet, read live as CSV on every question.
  • River flow forecast: Open-Meteo Flood API, daily.river_discharge (m³/s), GloFAS v4 data, today + next two days. Weather data by Open-Meteo.com (CC BY 4.0).
  • Weather warnings: Met Éireann, warning_IRELAND.json; counties matched by FIPS code (e.g. EI04 = Cork). Source: Met Éireann (licence based on CC BY 4.0). Warning text is shown unaltered.
  • Thresholds and history: 95th/99th percentiles and winter high-flow events from the same Open-Meteo/GloFAS series, 2016–2025 (summary.json).
  • Not covered: coastal and tidal flooding, road closures, insurance, individual homes. Contact the council's area office.
